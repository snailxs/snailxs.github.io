Energy Manipulation

BLENDER SUBSTANCE PAINTER UNITY MAYA C#
VIDEO DEMONSTRATION →

PROJECT TYPE

Virtual Reality Experience

DATE

April 2026

This was a personal project I worked on with a friend of mine. The goal was to create a virtual reality experience that revolved around glowing energy ball projectiles flying at the player. The player can grab these energy balls and throw them. If they manage to hit another flying projectile, then both energy balls will do a little explosion.

We both wanted to experiment with Unity's virtual reality capabilities, so we both worked on functionality and coding the experience mechanics in C# using Unity's scripting system as well as implementing the virtual reality hands to match with the controllers. It was a bit confusing at first trying to navigate Unity's virtual reality packages since my previous VR experience was in Unreal, and we frequently ran into issues with things not working or looking right with the headset on. Fortunately, we were able to overcome this thanks to sheer force of will and the package documentation.

I also utilized my previous experience as a technical artist to create the environment, lighting, particle effects, models, and textures for the experience. We had some frame rate issues at first, so I also worked on optimization of the projectile system and the models in order to ensure a smooth experience.
