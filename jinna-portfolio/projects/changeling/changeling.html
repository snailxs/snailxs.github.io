Changeling VR

BLENDER SUBSTANCE PAINTER UNREAL ENGINE MAYA OCULUS
OFFICIAL WEBSITE →

PROJECT TYPE

Virtual Reality Game

DATE

Summer 2025

ROLE

Technical Artist

Changeling VR is a first-person VR mystery game built in Unreal where you play as detective Aurelia Walker, who has magical abilities. I worked as a technical artist on this game for the summer of 2025, doing things like 3D modeling, texturing, rigging, environmental design, quality assurance, optimization, and debugging technical art related issues.

The level I worked primarily on was the Mother's Level, the first level the player encounters. For about two weeks I remade and refreshed the model for Toby, the baby of the family Aurelia was hired to help. His strange behavior is what prompted the job and is the basis of the game — long story short, he's been replaced with a "Changeling," and my task was to reimagine and recreate this Changeling baby. This involved heavily tweaking the existing model, texturing to give the child a tree-like appearance, working with LODs to help optimize, and retargeting the old rig onto the new baby model so it could still use all of its animations. This process was done in Maya, Adobe Substance Painter, and Blender.

After that, I focused on creating models and textures for various props and environmental objects, including low-poly, optimized clouds for the Mother's level, and assisted with its environmental design and layout. I also went through the project and cleaned up or consolidated unused assets to help with optimization and storage.

Towards the end of the summer, I was tasked with texturing a dragon model that appears as the final boss of Douglas's level, the second level of the game. This level was designed to look like a storybook, so I worked closely with the level's modeler and concept artist to texture the dragon so it looked good in 3D while still matching the concept artist's vision.

Throughout the summer I also created and ran playtests at the end of each sprint, gathering data to help shape the next sprint's goals. Changeling VR is available for early access on Steam — the official website link has more details.
